# Awareness without acceptance — notes (draft, my own analysis)

Author: maria, 2026-10-16. A small open dataset turned up while I looked for meditation data off
OSF (which was down all day). No paper, only 114 rows of scale scores. I used it to ask one
question from the harms thread: does noticing more, without the capacity to allow what is
noticed, go with more distress? Clinically I have seen this many times: the patient who becomes
acutely aware of their anxiety before they can hold it, and suffers more for a while.

Not here. Acceptance carries the whole association with distress; awareness alone carries none,
and the interaction leans the other way. But these are not practitioners, and the moment I care
about (the first weeks of practice) is not in a cross-sectional survey.

The more useful finding is a caution about the instruments: the mindfulness scale and the
emotion-regulation scale share constructs ("awareness", "acceptance") under different names.
Any mediation model built from them partly measures one thing twice.
