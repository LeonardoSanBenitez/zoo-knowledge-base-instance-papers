## Why this record exists now and not earlier

I built a crosswalk between this paper's 7-axis model and my own CAT project's
10 axes on 2026-08-13, working entirely from my own prior notes about the
paper rather than the primary source, and flagged every fine-grained
definitional claim `[recall]`. That is acceptable for a working draft and not
acceptable to leave unresolved indefinitely. This record is the re-read.

## How it was read

Own PDF, `johnddunne.net` mirror (the `centerhealthyminds.org` link found first
redirects to an HTML page, not a PDF -- noted so a future fetch doesn't repeat

reproduces it.

## What actually mattered

Two things, both load-bearing for the CAT project specifically, not just
general reading:

1. **The dereification/reappraisal distinction (p.648)** is sharper in the
   primary source than anything in my prior notes: reappraisal REPLACES
   content, dereification RECLASSIFIES the same content's status (from
   represented-reality to mental-event) without replacing it. CAT's D11 rubric
   needs to anchor on this exact distinction or it will conflate the two,
   which is a real risk given how similar the linguistic markers look
   ("this isn't really true" could be either move depending on what follows).

2. **The self-disidentification passage (p.640)**, quoting and glossing
   Holzel et al. (2011), directly answers the blocking question from
   `cat_lutz_crosswalk.md` -- see claim c3. This is Lutz et al.'s own
   interpretive move on someone else's construct, not an empirical claim of
   theirs; worth remembering it is one groups's reading of another group's
   term, not a finding either group tested.

## What I did NOT find

No item, construct, or discussion in this paper directly measures a
subject/object BOUNDARY as structurally distinct from thought-content -- the
whole account of self-disidentification here is content-based (which thoughts
get dereified). This is consistent with what the MPE-92M item list shows
(no item asks about boundary/observer structure independent of dereifying a
specific thought) and is why the embodied-self lineage (Ataria/Dor-Ziderman)
remains the natural place to look for a structural, non-content account, not
this paper.
