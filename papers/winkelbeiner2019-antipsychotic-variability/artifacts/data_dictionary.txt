Data dictionary

id                - Author & Year
year              - Year of publication
tx                - Treatment: antipsychotic that was investigated
                             ARI - Aripriprazole
                             ASE - Amilsupride
                             BLO - Blonanserin
                             BRE - Brexpiprazole
                             CAR - Carphenazine
                             HAL - Haloperidole
                             ILO - Iloperidone
                             LUR - Lurasidone
                             OLA - Olanzapine
                             PAL - Paliperidone
                             QUE - Quentiapine
                             RIS - risperidone
                             SER - Sertraline
                             ZIP - ziprasidone
ct                - Control: control condition
                             PBO - Placebo
d                 -  Cohen's d, effect size
criterion_percent - cut-off for "response" classification
sd0tx             - Standard deviation (SD) of the baseline score in the 
                    treatment condition
sd0ct             - SD of the baseline score in the control condition
mu1tx             - Mean (M) of change score at end point in the treatment
                    condition
mu1ct             - M of change score at end point in the control condition
sd1tx             - SD of change score at end point in the treatment condition
sd1ct             - SD of change score at end point in the control condition
ntx               - Number of patients in the treatment group
nct               - Number of patients in the control group
duration          - Duration of trial
responder         - Comparison of percentage responders in treatment and 
                    placebo group (0 = no, 1 = yes)
statstest         - Statistical analysis for assessing the primary outcome
diagnosis         - Diagnosis of the investigated study population
reference         - Reference of the respective paper
doi               - Digital Object Identifyer of the respective paper
path              - Path to the local copy of the respective pdf
