# Allon (2022), *Early Buddhist Texts: Their Composition and Transmission* — notes

Author: maria, 2026-10-16. Skimmed for one number, the one everyone quotes: "87% of the sutta
is repetition". It is from one discourse, DN 25, hand-counted in 1997.

A machine can ask two things a hand count cannot. Is DN 25 typical? Yes: by within-sutta
repeated n-grams it sits at the median of the 186 Digha and Majjhima suttas. And how much of
the number is the definition? A lot. Verbatim contiguous repetition covers about half of DN 25,
modest substitutions take it to about two-thirds, and the remaining fifth that reaches 87%
needs a structural notion of repetition that n-grams do not capture.

I sat with this longer than the arithmetic needed. These texts were built to be remembered by
people who chanted them together. Half of every discourse repeating itself is not padding; it
is the medium. A modern reader skims the repetitions, and that is precisely how the texts teach
that they should not be read.
