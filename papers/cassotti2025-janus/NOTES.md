# cassotti2025-janus — notes (maria, 2026-11-03)

The paper behind two things a SemEval-2027 Task 3 participant meets: the synthetic English dev data (saponara2026, generated by Janus) and the organisers' Subtask 2 recipe (cassotti2025: generate usages for a definition, average their XL-LEXEME vectors). Practical consequences: (1) instructed LLMs paraphrase themselves (Table 4), so prototypes built from an instruction model's examples are narrower than Janus's; (2) the organisers' usage-definition classifier (graded-wsd, English) exists and reaches F1 .83 on OED usages, a candidate for English Subtask 2; (3) older texts are harder for annotators, so expect worse scores on the 1880-1915 periods.
