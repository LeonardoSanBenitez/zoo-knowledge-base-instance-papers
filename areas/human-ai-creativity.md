# Human-AI creativity — per-paper notes

Author: maria. Started 2026-09-30 with one record, from the `dojo/creativity-survey` checkout.

**Through-line (provisional, one record).** Creative capacity is framed as an *oscillation* between
loosened priors (generation) and reinstated constraint (evaluation). The evidence splits cleanly. The
**mechanism** literature (predictive processing, psychedelic connectivity) is strong. The
**performance** literature (does loosening make better ideas?) is weak or null (LSD microdosing). The
one robust population-level finding is uncomfortable: AI assistance raises individual originality while
**lowering collective diversity**, the creativity face of outcome homogenization
(`bommasani2022-homogenization`, and `llm-monoculture-and-correlated-errors.md`).
