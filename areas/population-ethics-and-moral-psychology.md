# Population ethics and moral psychology

Author: maria. Started 2026-10-16. Records: `caviola2022-population-ethical-intuitions`,
`schubert2019-psychology-existential-risk`.
Adjacent: `karger2023-xpt` (longtermist numbers as elicited quantities), the `wellbeing-and-income`
area (what a well-being scale measures), `maria2026-happiness-income-spread` (centre vs shape).

**Scope note.** Not *which* axiology is right. What lay judgments about populations are, how they
are measured, and how much of an argument can stand on them. Philosophers cite this literature in
two directions -- "intuitions favour totalism, so longtermism" and "intuitions are averagist and
incoherent, so discount them" -- and both uses need the numbers to mean what the abstract says.

## The lead (one record so far; treat as a hypothesis about the field)

**Lay population axiology, as measured, is mostly a quality judgment with a weak, saturating
sensitivity to numbers of happy people, a strong sensitivity to numbers of suffering people, and a
mixture of types under every mean.**

- **Suffering outweighs happiness, as a median of a mixture.** With intensities stipulated equal,
  37% weigh them exactly equally and the rest require more happiness; the median trade ratio is
  1.5:1 (3:1 at extreme intensities). `caviola2022#c1`.
- **Creating people is not neutral for most -- but for about one in five it is.** The procreation
  asymmetry (unhappy addition bad, happy addition neutral) is held by 7.6%, and its mirror image by
  6.4%: the population is symmetric at the level of individual types, not just of means. `#c3`.
- **Numbers of happy people saturate fast.** 10,000x more happy people recovers about a quarter of
  what a 20-point fall in average costs; past a million, each factor of ten is worth ~0.02 of a
  7-point scale. `#c5`. For a longtermist argument from astronomical numbers, lay axiology gives
  almost no purchase.
- **Averagism is not innumeracy.** It is as strong among the few who computed that the totals
  were equal. `#c5`.

## The method trap this area has already shown

**Valence-mirrored designs with one arm reverse-coded.** If the unhappy arm is reversed before
comparison, any common directional preference (here: for smaller populations) shows up as a
valence asymmetry, growing wherever the common preference grows. The only clean check is a
neutral arm that is randomised separately; a neutral block asked after the valenced block is
post-treatment and contaminated (in Caviola 3a: identical items, arm-dependent answers,
p = 2e-6). `caviola2022#c4` is `not-identifiable` for this reason. Expect the same structure in
any happy/unhappy, gain/loss or help/harm mirrored vignette study.

## Extinction (added 2026-10-16, `schubert2019-psychology-existential-risk`)

Unprompted, only 19-24% judge the step from 80% killed to 100% killed worse than the step from 0 to
80%; free texts show people counting deaths. Make the long future salient and it doubles; describe
it as utopian and it reaches 67-77%. Robust to the forced-choice format and to the ranking filter
(both checked from the data). Same shape as `caviola2022#c5`: **the size of the future is not a
lay intuition; it is a response to how the future is described.** The instrument measures whether
extinction's premium exceeds 60% of humanity's lives -- a threshold, not a zero point.
